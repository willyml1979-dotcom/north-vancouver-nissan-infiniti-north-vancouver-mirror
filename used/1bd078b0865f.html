2023 Ford F-150 Tremor SuperCrew 4x4 — Off-Road Capability Meets Everyday Performance

The 2023 Ford F-150 Tremor 4x4 delivers an impressive combination of powerful performance, rugged off-road capability and everyday versatility. Its 3.5L EcoBoost twin-turbocharged V6 engine produces 400 horsepower and 500 lb-ft of torque, paired with a 10-speed automatic transmission and four-wheel drive for impressive acceleration and confident performance. Equipped with the Tremor Off-Road Package, this F-150 is designed to tackle challenging terrain while delivering the comfort and capability you want in a full-size pickup.

Finished in Agate Black Metallic, this SuperCrew Tremor makes a bold statement with its aggressive styling, 18-inch aluminum-alloy wheels and 33-inch all-terrain tires. The specialized Tremor off-road suspension and electronic-locking rear differential provide added capability when the pavement ends. Inside, enjoy heated and ventilated front seats, a heated steering wheel, premium B&O audio and modern connectivity. Whether you're heading to the worksite, towing your toys or exploring off the beaten path, this F-150 Tremor is ready for the journey.
