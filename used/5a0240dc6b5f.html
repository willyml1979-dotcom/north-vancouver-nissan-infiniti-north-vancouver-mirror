Experience refined luxury and exhilarating performance in this Audi S6 quattro. Its 2.9L twin-turbocharged V6 produces 444 horsepower and 443 lb-ft of torque, paired with an 8-speed Tiptronic automatic transmission and quattro all-wheel drive for powerful acceleration and confident handling. Mild-hybrid technology helps deliver smooth, responsive performance.

The Black Optics Package adds a bold, sporty appearance with dark exterior accents and distinctive styling. The Driver Assistance Package provides advanced driver-support technology to help make highway driving more comfortable. Inside, the Full Leather Package extends premium leather across the upper and lower dashboard and centre console for a more luxurious cabin finish.

Additional highlights include Audi Phone Box with Qi wireless charging and signal boosting, a heated three-spoke steering wheel with paddle shifters, and rear side airbags with illuminated seatbelt buckles. Combining executive comfort with S model performance, this S6 is designed for drivers who want both refinement and excitement.
