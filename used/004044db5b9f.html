2024 Honda CR-V Sport AWD — Sporty Style, Everyday Versatility

The 2024 Honda CR-V Sport AWD combines modern styling, impressive versatility and everyday comfort in a well-equipped compact SUV. Designed for commuters, families and weekend adventurers, the CR-V delivers a confident driving experience with a spacious interior, thoughtful technology and the added traction of Honda’s Real Time AWD system.

Powered by a 1.5L turbocharged 4-cylinder engine producing 190 horsepower, paired with a continuously variable automatic transmission, the CR-V Sport AWD offers responsive performance and a comfortable ride. Its bold exterior styling, black sport accents and refined interior make it an excellent choice for drivers looking for practicality with a sporty edge.
