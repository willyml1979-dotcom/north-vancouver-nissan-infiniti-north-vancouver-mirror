2022 DODGE CHARGER R/T — THE SOUND AND POWER OF A TRUE V8

The 2022 Dodge Charger R/T brings classic American muscle-car performance together with the practicality of a four-door sedan. Powered by a 5.7L HEMI V8 engine producing 370 horsepower and 395 lb-ft of torque, it delivers strong acceleration and an unmistakable V8 driving experience. Paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission, the Charger offers responsive performance and smooth shifting.
