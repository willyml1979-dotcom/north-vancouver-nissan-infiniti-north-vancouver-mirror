2025 Ford Explorer ST 4WD — High-Performance Power Meets Premium Family Luxury

The 2025 Ford Explorer ST 4WD delivers an exciting combination of turbocharged performance, aggressive styling and premium three-row versatility. Its 3.0L EcoBoost twin-turbocharged V6 engine produces 400 horsepower and 415 lb-ft of torque, paired with a 10-speed automatic transmission and four-wheel drive for impressive acceleration and confident performance in a variety of driving conditions. With its sport-tuned suspension, paddle shifters and advanced Terrain Management System, the Explorer ST brings performance-inspired driving to a versatile family SUV.

Finished in Grey, this Explorer ST combines bold exterior styling with an upscale and technology-focused interior. Enjoy heated and ventilated front seats, heated second-row seats, a heated steering wheel and a panoramic Vista Roof with power shade. The impressive 13.2-inch touchscreen, 12.3-inch digital instrument cluster and premium Bang & Olufsen sound system bring modern technology and entertainment to every journey. With three-row seating, power-folding rear seats and advanced driver-assistance technology, this Explorer is ready for family adventures, daily commutes and weekend getaways.
